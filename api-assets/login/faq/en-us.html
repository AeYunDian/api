Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is AyAccount?

AyAccount is the credential for logging in to AeYunDian's software products, i.e., the AY account.

You can quickly create an AY account by selecting AyAccount in the login options within Yundian's software, and then log in successfully.

The user email is the unique ID of the AY account. You can view the email associated with your account on the AY account official website.

2. Cannot log in to your AY account?

Scenario 1: Forgot password

You can click "Forgot Password" on the login page, verify your identity, and then reset a new login password.

Scenario 2: Identity verification

Our system has detected a risk with your account. To protect your account security, please complete the verification as prompted. After successful verification, you can log in.

Scenario 3: Email deactivated

If the email bound to your account cannot receive messages normally, you can contact us via these contact methods. After verification, you can change the bound email.

After rebinding, you can log in with the new email.

3. How to protect your account security?

1) Please provide accurate information, which can serve as important proof of account ownership;

2) Enable New Device Verification – when your account logs in to client applications on a new device using a password, verification is required;

3) Bind an email address, which can be used as a verification method for account recovery;

4) If the email bound to your account is changed by someone else, you can log in to AY Account (online.undz.cn) and activate Login Restriction – until the restriction is lifted, no one can use that account;

5) Good security awareness reduces ownership disputes from the start. Please keep your account safe and prevent others from using it.

4. How to recover a stolen account?

1) If your account password is leaked, you can verify your identity via the bound email and reset your password;

2) If the bound email is changed by someone else and you cannot recover it through normal procedures, you can contact us via these contact methods for verification. After verification, we will help you change the bound email;

3) If your account is being used by someone else, you can log in to AY Account (online.undz.cn) and enable Login Restriction – until lifted, no one can use the account.

Other Questions

1. Why is security verification required when logging in?

1) Our system has detected a risk with your account. To protect your account, please complete identity verification as prompted;

2) AY Account (online.undz.cn) – if New Device Verification is enabled, logging in to games from a new device requires verification.

2. What is New Device Verification?

New Device Verification is an extra layer of security for AY Account (online.undz.cn) . When logging in from a new device, your bound email must be verified, otherwise you cannot log in. New Device Verification is turned off by default.

If the above does not resolve your issue, please contact us via aeyundian@outlook.com or these contact methods.
